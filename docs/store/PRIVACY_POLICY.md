# Privacy Policy for Sahaba Companions (رفقاء الصحابة)

**Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Application ID:** `com.aslmmovic.qurancompanion`  
**Repository:** [https://github.com/Aslmmon/CompanionApp](https://github.com/Aslmmon/CompanionApp)

---

## 🛡️ Core Commitment
**Sahaba Companions** is designed with a strict privacy-first philosophy. We do **NOT** collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). The application operates 100% offline.

---

## 1. Information Collection & Usage
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, or provide personal details (name, email, phone number).
* **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not track or collect your location, IP address, device IDs, or behavioral telemetry.
* **No Analytics / Telemetry:** The app contains zero analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.).
* **Zero Advertisements:** The app is 100% ad-free. No advertising identifiers or ad trackers are present.

---

## 2. Device Permissions
* **`POST_NOTIFICATIONS` (Local Daily Reminders):**
  This permission is used exclusively to schedule local on-device alarms for your daily reminder (e.g. Post-Fajr 06:00 AM, Morning 08:00 AM, Evening 08:00 PM). Notification triggers are scheduled and fired locally by Android AlarmManager/WorkManager. No data is transmitted to external notification servers.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage
All user preferences and progress remain exclusively on your device:
* Language preference (English / العربية).
* Selected theme (Light, Dark, Mushaf Parchment, Emerald Andalus).
* Daily journey completion status & weekly habit streak.
* Scheduled reminder time.

Uninstalling the application completely erases this local data.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)
Our application contains educational Islamic history suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.
