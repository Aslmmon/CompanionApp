Privacy Policy

Sahaba Companions • رفقاء الصحابة

Effective Date: September 2026

Application: Sahaba Companions (com.aslmmovic.qurancompanion)

🛡️ Core Privacy Commitment: Sahaba Companions is designed with a strict privacy-first philosophy. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. The app operates 100% offline.

1. Information Collection and Usage

We believe in absolute privacy. When you use Sahaba Companions:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, sign in, or provide an email address or phone number.
  • No Personal Data Collected: We do not track or record your name, location, contacts, IP address, device identifiers, or usage patterns.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The application does not contain third-party tracking SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.).
  • No Advertisements: The application is completely ad-free. There are no advertising SDKs or data profiling trackers.

2. Device Permissions & Notifications

The app requests the following optional permission:

  • Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to schedule local, on-device daily reminders (e.g., Post-Fajr 6:00 AM, Morning 8:00 AM, or Evening 8:00 PM) to help you maintain your daily reading habit. All notification scheduling happens entirely locally on your device; no data is sent over the internet. You can turn off reminders at any time in the app settings or system settings.

3. Local Data Storage

To provide a smooth offline experience, the app stores minimal preference data locally on your device's internal storage:

  • Selected language (English or Arabic).
  • Selected visual theme (e.g., Dark Mode or Mushaf Parchment).
  • Daily journey completion status and weekly streak tracker dots.
  • Reminder time preferences.

This data remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted to any external servers or third parties. Uninstalling the app clears this local data.

4. Third-Party Services

The application does not transmit data to any third-party APIs or external service providers. All educational stories and biographical content are bundled offline within the application package.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is safe for users of all ages, including children. Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, we comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact us via our repository issue tracker at GitHub: Aslmmon/CompanionApp.
