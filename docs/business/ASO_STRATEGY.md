**Application ID:** `com.aslmmovic.qurancompanion`  
**Target Platform:** Google Play Store (Android-first, iOS-ready)  
**Primary Languages:** English (`en-US`, `en-GB`, International), Arabic (`ar`, `ar-SA`, `ar-EG`)  
**Core Value Proposition:** A tranquil, 10-minute daily habit exploring the lives, virtues, and timeless lessons of the noble Sahaba — 100% offline and zero advertisements.

## 1. Executive 
Summary & Market Positioning

### 1.1 The Market Opportunity
The Islamic lifestyle and spirituality app landscape is dominated by complex, ad-heavy "super-apps" (prayer times, full Quran readers, compasses, tasbeeh counters). While useful, they suffer from three major user pain points:
1. **Severe Ad Fatigue & Privacy Violations**: Users are increasingly frustrated by intrusive full-screen video ads and data-sharing controversies inside spiritual applications.
2. **Decision Fatigue & Information Overload**: Encyclopedic apps dump thousands of pages of raw text, leaving modern Muslims unsure where to start.
3. **Weak Daily Habit Formation**: Apps lack structured, bite-sized daily curricula designed to be completed in under 10 minutes.

### 1.2 Sahaba Companions Positioning (The "Blinkist + Duolingo for Islamic History")
Sahaba Companions targets the unserved need for **intentional, calm, bite-sized Islamic reflection**:
* **One Story Per Day**: Zero browsing fatigue. Open the app, and today's lesson is waiting.
* **The 5-Step Micro-Learning Journey**: Introduction ➔ Story ➔ Key Lessons ➔ Reflection ➔ Today's Action.
* **Sacred Focus (Ad-Free & Offline)**: No banners, no trackers, no sign-up barrier, and 100% functional without an internet connection.

## 7. Ratings, Reviews & Social Proof Strategy

### 7.1 In-App Review Prompt Strategy
* **Timing is Critical**: Never prompt on first launch or during onboarding.
* **The "Delight Moment" Trigger**: Trigger Google Play In-App Review API (`ReviewManager`) exclusively when:
  1. The user completes **Day 7 of their streak**, OR
  2. The user has completed **10 total journeys**, immediately on the `CompletionScreen` after the "Alhamdulillah" card is shown.
* **Negative Feedback Gate**: Before invoking the native Play Store dialog, present a lightweight satisfaction check:
  - *"Are you enjoying your daily journeys with the Sahaba?"*
  - If **Yes** ➔ Launch native Google Play Review modal.
  - If **Not really** ➔ Provide an internal email feedback link (`support@aslmmovic.com`) to catch bugs privately.

### 7.2 Review Response Protocols (Developer Support)
* **Positive 5-Star Reviews**:
  > *"Jazakallahu Khairan for your kind words! May Allah bless your learning and grant you consistency in walking in the footsteps of the noble Sahaba. If you have any suggestions, we'd love to hear from you."*
* **Bug Reports (1–3 Stars)**:
  > *"Assalamu Alaikum. We apologize for the issue! We are an independent team dedicated to making this the purest, ad-free Islamic app. Please reach out to us at [support email] with your device model so we can fix this immediately in our next update."*
